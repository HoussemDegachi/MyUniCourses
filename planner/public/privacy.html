Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

uschedule.ai helps university students build class schedules around the professors, times and days they want. It was built at Hack the Hill III. This page explains what information it uses, why, and how to remove it.

The short version
  • You can use the planner without an account.
  • We don't keep a database of users, sell data, show ads or use tracking.
  • If you connect Google Calendar, we only create a new calendar for your classes. We never read, change or delete your other calendars or events.

What we use, and why

Your courses and preferences. The courses you pick, your settings, and anything you type into the preferences box are sent to our server to build your schedules. The preferences text is sent to Google's Gemini AI so it can turn your words into settings. Facts about the finished schedules (such as class times and professor ratings) are also sent to Gemini to write the explanation. None of this is linked to your identity, and what you type is not stored.

Calendars you import. If you import a calendar file, it is read in your browser. Only the day, time and title of weekly events are sent to our server, so classes can avoid those times. They are not stored.

Your account, if you sign in. Sign-in is handled by Auth0 using your Google account. Auth0 receives your name, email address and profile picture, and we use them only to show that you're signed in and to add your schedule to your calendar.

Google Calendar, if you allow it. When you sign in, Google asks whether uschedule.ai may access your calendar. Auth0 keeps the access Google grants, and our server uses it only when you press Add to Google Calendar. It then creates one new calendar named after the term and adds your classes to it. We don't read, change or delete any other calendar or event, and the access is never sent to your browser.

Your device. Your browser stores your light or dark theme choice and, if you sign in, your sign-in session. Nothing else.

Google user data

uschedule.ai's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to add your schedule to your calendar, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

Services we rely on

  • Auth0 for sign-in and keeping your Google Calendar access.
  • Google for sign-in, Google Calendar, and the Gemini AI. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use submitted text to improve its products, so please don't type personal information into the preferences box.
  • Rate My Professors for public professor ratings. We send only professor names, never anything about you.
  • The university's public class listings for course sections and times.

What we keep

We keep temporary copies of public course listings, professor ratings and AI-written schedule explanations so the site stays fast. They contain no personal information. Auth0 keeps your account until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Remove uschedule.ai's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the calendar we created from Google Calendar at any time. Removing it deletes every class we added.
  • Ask us to delete your account, or ask anything about this policy, by emailing privacy@uschedule.ai.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
